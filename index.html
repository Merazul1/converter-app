Currency Exchange Rates & Global Market Insights

The foreign exchange (forex) market is the largest and most liquid financial market in the world, with over $7.5 trillion traded daily. Exchange rates determine how much of one national currency can be exchanged for another based on macroeconomic fundamentals, central bank monetary policy, and international trade flows.

Currency Conversion Methods & Formula

Calculations on CurrenFlow use the true institutional mid-market exchange rate without retail markups: Target Amount = Source Amount × Live Rate. To convert in reverse, use Source Amount = Target Amount × Inverse Rate.

Historical Exchange Rates & Ranges

Major currency pairs such as USD/EUR, USD/CAD, and GBP/EUR trade within historical economic bands influenced by quarterly GDP announcements, monthly employment figures, and transatlantic trade sentiment.

Central Bank Policies & Interest Rates

Benchmark borrowing costs set by the US Federal Reserve (Fed), European Central Bank (ECB), and Bank of England (BoE) drive institutional capital flows, creating daily exchange rate appreciation and depreciation trends.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the mid-market exchange rate?

The mid-market rate (also called the interbank rate) is the exact midpoint between the buy and sell rates on global currency markets. It is the real, fair rate without any retail bank markup or conversion spread.

Privacy Policy for CurrenFlow

Last updated: October 2026

At CurrenFlow, accessible from https://currenflow.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by CurrenFlow and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

CurrenFlow does not require user registration or accounts. We do not collect names, passwords, phone numbers, or credit card information. Conversions happen client-side in your web browser.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like many other websites, CurrenFlow uses cookies to store information including visitors' preferences, such as selected base and target currencies, to provide an enhanced user experience upon return visits.

3. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie & Third-Party Advertising

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to currenflow.com and other sites on the internet. Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. Third-Party Privacy Policies

CurrenFlow's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. We advise you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information.

5. GDPR and CCPA Data Protection Rights

We want to make sure you are fully aware of all your data protection rights. Every user is entitled to request access to personal data, request correction of inaccurate data, and request erasure under applicable law.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, please contact us by email at support@currenflow.com.

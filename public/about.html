How We Calculate Exchange Rates

CurrenFlow calculates exchange rates using the Mid-Market Rate (also known as the interbank rate or spot rate). Here is our multi-tiered verification standard:

  • Official Central Bank Reference Feeds: Primary benchmark data is synchronized daily against official rates published by the European Central Bank (ECB) and corresponding national monetary authorities.
  • Commercial Interbank Cross-Checks: Real-time price actions from wholesale FX liquidity pools are referenced every few minutes to ensure accurate reflection of intraday market movements.
  • Cross-Currency Triangular Arbitrage Checks: For exotic currency pairs without a direct trading market, rates are algorithmically routed through USD and EUR base pivots to prevent artificial discrepancies.
What is the "Mid-Market" Rate?

The mid-market rate is the halfway point between what currency buyers are offering (the bid price) and what currency sellers are willing to accept (the ask price) in global institutional markets. Because this rate does not include any commercial markup, it represents the only fair, unbiased standard for comparing money transfer services.
